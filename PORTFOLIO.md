## Professional Case Studies

### 1. Multimodal Perception Data Quality and Team Operations

**Context:** Computer-vision and autonomy workflows depend on consistent image, video, and 3D data, with clear instructions and dependable review.

**My contribution:** Translated technical needs into annotation standards, taxonomies, examples, review criteria, onboarding material, and handoff practices. Led day-to-day technical operations and coaching across a 30+ contributor organization. Used measures such as intersection-over-union (IoU), inter-annotator agreement, targeted audits, and issue escalation to identify ambiguity and quality risk.

**Impact:** Improved consistency between annotation work and downstream needs, made quality issues easier to identify and resolve, reduced avoidable QA returns, and made handoffs more predictable. The public account intentionally omits internal acceptance thresholds, dataset volumes, customer details, and proprietary taxonomies.

**Capabilities demonstrated:** Quality operations · multimodal data · taxonomy and guideline design · coaching · issue triage · stakeholder communication.

### 2. Simulation-Data Readiness and Evidence-Based Evaluation

**Context:** Synthetic data is useful only when generated outputs are valid, traceable, sufficiently complete, and evaluated with appropriate comparisons.

**My contribution:** Helped translate model and operational questions into scenario plans, coverage requirements, delivery priorities, and acceptance checks. Traced data through generation, validation, manifests, artifacts, and downstream handoff. Investigated incomplete outputs and workflow failures using schema, provenance, logs, and run-state evidence. Compared experiments against explicit baselines and preserved mixed results instead of presenting a single favorable metric as a universal improvement.

**Impact:** Made readiness and limitations more visible before downstream use, improved the quality of investigation and handoff decisions, and helped prevent incomplete runs from being mistaken for usable deliverables. Model results were mixed; I do not claim a general performance gain.

**Capabilities demonstrated:** Synthetic-data operations · dataset readiness · provenance · schema and completeness checks · experiment design · failure analysis · careful technical reporting.

### 3. Reporting and Workflow Automation

**Context:** Repetitive reporting and fragmented tracking made it harder for operations and quality teams to see current status and act consistently.

**My contribution:** Built and maintained reporting and workflow automation using Python, SQL, Databricks, APIs, JavaScript, and Google Apps Script. Consolidated recurring reporting and quality checks, parameterized workflows, organized structured outputs, and improved stakeholder visibility.

**Impact:** Reduced manual coordination and repeated reporting effort, shortened reporting turnaround, and made recurring information easier to inspect. I do not publish a percentage reduction because the public evidence set does not include a reproducible measurement baseline.

**Capabilities demonstrated:** Data transformation · automation · API integration · operational analytics · lightweight internal-tool design · requirements ownership and validation.

### 4. UAV Operations, Maintenance, and Field Enablement

**Context:** Field operations require equipment readiness, disciplined procedures, clear communication, and careful response to technical issues.

**My contribution:** Supported UAV launch and recovery, readiness checks, preventive and corrective maintenance, troubleshooting, field setup, system installation, operator training, and procedure compliance. Coordinated across operators and technical stakeholders and escalated issues when further engineering attention was needed.

**Impact:** Helped teams operate more consistently in the field and supported training and site readiness. This is hands-on operational experience, not a claim of licensed aircraft-certifying authority or formal Part-66/Part-145 approval.

**Capabilities demonstrated:** UAV operations · equipment readiness · practical troubleshooting · field deployment · operator training · safety-minded escalation.

### 5. Telecommunications Field Testing

**Context:** Network performance and site readiness need to be checked in real operating conditions, not inferred only from desk-based plans.

**My contribution:** Conducted site surveys and physical site checks, drive tests, carrier benchmarking, functionality tests, coverage analysis, and technical reporting. The work combined field observation, navigation, data interpretation, and practical maintenance and optimization tasks.

**Impact:** Produced field evidence that supported assessment of coverage and operational parameters.

**Capabilities demonstrated:** RF/telecommunications foundation · field testing · measurement · data interpretation · technical reporting.

### 6. Solar Field Support and Customer-Facing Technical Service

**Solar field work:** Retrofitted solar-panel control circuits and supported farmer sensitization. This combined basic electrical troubleshooting and practical installation support with clear communication in field settings.

**Customer support:** At a public-service help desk, guided customers through computer-related tasks and account/profile reviews. This strengthened my ability to explain technical steps patiently and work with users who have different levels of digital confidence.

**Capabilities demonstrated:** Electronics · solar systems · field service · user support · practical training · communication.

## Academic and Independent Projects

### Voice-Controlled Wheelchair Proof of Concept

For my university engineering capstone, a three-person student team built a small prototype connecting voice input to microcontroller logic and motor control. The prototype demonstrated basic forward, backward, left, right, and stop commands. The project also documented speech-recognition limitations and the gap between a tabletop proof of concept and a load-bearing mobility device. It was not a certified or user-ready wheelchair.

**My learning:** Embedded systems · electronics · system decomposition · input-to-actuation control · prototype testing · honest safety and scale limitations.

### Concrete-Strength Prediction Coursework

In a WorldQuant University project, I prepared tabular materials data and built a deep-learning regression workflow. The work included preprocessing, feature scaling, model tuning, and error analysis. I present this as coursework, not as a deployed engineering service.
